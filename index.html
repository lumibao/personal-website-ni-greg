this website took longer than I expected

I thought making a website would take one afternoon. It took three weekends, and half of that was me fixing the spacing between sections.

But I like it now. It's not the prettiest thing, but every part of it is something I typed myself, and I understand all of it. That feels better than something fancy that I couldn't explain.

lesson learned: margins are important and also evil.
