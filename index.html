I'm Greg, a student from the Philippines. This is my personal website.

It has three parts I actually use: a short page about me, a few photos, and some short notes. The last section is for our ICT subject, where we were asked to make a resume, a calendar, a banner, a background, a logo, and a template.

I built this with plain HTML and CSS. No framework, no template. I'm still learning, so it stays simple on purpose.
