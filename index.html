About Me

the person behind the site

Short biography

Hi! I'm Greg, a student who enjoys expressing myself through the things I create and the things I love. I made this website so my friends, classmates, and anyone who visits can get a glimpse of who I really am — not just a name on a list, but a person with stories, interests, and a creative side.

I believe a personal website should feel welcoming and easy to explore. So I kept this space simple and honest: my story, my favorite things, my photos and creative work, and my personal reflections. If you're reading this, thanks for stopping by — I hope you enjoy looking around.

What I value

  • Honesty — being real about who I am and what I feel.
  • Creativity — making things, even imperfect ones, is how I learn.
  • Curiosity — always asking questions and trying new things.
  • Respect — listening to others and keeping this space welcoming.

Nov 2026Reflection

Why I made this website

I wanted a space that feels like me — not just a school requirement, but a place where I can share my story in my own voice...

I wanted a space that feels like me — not just a school requirement, but a place where I can share my story in my own voice. Through this site, I can show my favorite things, my photos, my creative work, and the thoughts I usually keep to myself.

Writing this made me realize how much of my identity is in the small things: the music I listen to, the art I make, the people I spend time with. I hope visitors leave this page understanding a little more about what makes me unique.

Oct 2026Idea

Small things that make my day better

A good song, a finished sketch, a game with friends — these small moments add up to a good day...

A good song, a finished sketch, a game with friends — these small moments add up to a good day. I don't need big events to feel happy. Sometimes it's just finishing something I started and thinking, "I made that."

That's why I keep creating, even when my work isn't perfect. The process itself is the reward.

Sep 2026Reflection

What I'm learning about myself

Building this site taught me that expressing myself is a skill — and like any skill, it gets better with practice...

Building this site taught me that expressing myself is a skill — and like any skill, it gets better with practice. At first, I didn't know what to write. But the more honest I was, the easier it became.

I want to keep this space updated and keep sharing who I am, one post at a time.
